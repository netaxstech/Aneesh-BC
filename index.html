About Netsol Kuwait

Netsol Kuwait designs, installs, and maintains IT and ELV systems for commercial, residential, and industrial clients across Kuwait — from a single CCTV camera to a fully networked, multi-site security and AV infrastructure.

Every project is handled by in-house engineers, from initial site survey through installation, commissioning, and after-sales support — so clients deal with one accountable team from start to finish.

We serve Kuwait nationwide, with a focus on reliable, standards-compliant work and long-term support relationships rather than one-off installs.
